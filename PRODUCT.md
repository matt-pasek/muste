# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One learner: a native Polish speaker, fluent in English, studying Finnish at A1 level with the Suomen Mestari 1 textbook. They study on an iPad with an Apple Pencil, usually in short focused sessions at home, writing in the book, tapping words for explanations, shadowing sentences out loud and talking with a tutor. Long vowels (tuuli vs tuli) are their known blind spot.

## Product Purpose

muste turns a textbook PDF into a patient tutor: ink on the page, explanations on tap, spoken audio, pronunciation feedback and a conversation partner who teaches. Success is more Finnish spoken out loud with less friction, and mistakes that feel safe to make.

## Brand Personality

Calm, warm, alive. A quiet Nordic room with one living presence in it. Unhurried and gentle with mistakes; the warmth comes from the voice, the words and Virva's movement, not from decoration.

## Anti-references

- Generic chatbot UI: ChatGPT-style bubbles stacked over an input bar.
- Gamified language apps: Duolingo streaks, mascots, confetti, XP.
- Flashy AI-orb demos: a glowing orb on black with nothing to do.

## Design Principles

- The book and the voice come first; chrome recedes until it is needed.
- One living thing per screen. Virva is the presence in the conversation; everything else stays quiet so it can breathe.
- Mistakes are welcome. Corrections are gentle, specific and always paired with how to say it right.
- Speak, don't type. Every flow favours saying Finnish out loud over reading about it.
- Built with usva: role tokens, subpath imports, the intensity of each layer respected.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on every text role (usva's ink and muted). Respect prefers-reduced-motion: Virva and animations fall back to static or crossfades. Touch targets sized for fingers on an iPad. Every spoken line also has its text and English translation on screen.
